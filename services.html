our Mission

HatBazar Limited aims to establish Nepal’s first nationwide supermarket built on the foundation of direct public investment, making high-quality products accessible to every citizen at affordable prices. Beyond retail, our mission is to strengthen the national production system by providing direct market access to local farmers, small and medium enterprises (SMEs), and domestic producers. Within the next five years, we plan to open state-of-the-art showrooms in all major cities, ensuring fair pricing without compromising on quality and prioritizing indigenous and locally produced goods.

Building a Self-Reliant Nepal

By connecting producers and consumers directly, HatBazar Limited reduces import dependence, promotes domestic production, and creates sustainable economic opportunities nationwide.

learn more

our Vision

HatBazar Limited envisions becoming Nepal’s first nationwide, participatory retail supermarket network — delivering transparent, high-quality, and affordable shopping experiences to consumers from all segments of society. Our vision is to build a trusted, Nepalese-owned retail platform that connects producers and consumers through a fair and efficient marketplace.

We aim to expand an integrated retail network across all major cities of the country, creating opportunities where local products reach the national market and national products access international markets. Through this model, Nepal will gradually transition from an import-dependent economy to a production-driven economy.

Empowering Nepal Through Retail

A nationwide supermarket network built by the people, for the people — driving production, opportunity, and economic independence.
